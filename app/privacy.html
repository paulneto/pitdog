PITDOG privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026

PitDog is a pit-wall app for amateur endurance race teams, run by one person (Paul Neto) as a personal project. It isn’t a business, it has no ads, and it never sells or shares your data.

What PitDog stores

  • On your device: your team, cars, races, race logs and settings, in the browser’s storage. You can export or delete it from the app’s Log tab.
  • If you sign in (team sync): your Google account’s name, email address and account ID, which your team you’re in, your role, and the team data you sync (plans, race logs, roster and cars).
  • Weather: the app asks Open-Meteo for the forecast at your track’s coordinates. No personal data is sent.

Sign-in with Google

PitDog only asks Google for your basic profile (name and email) so you can sign in. It doesn’t access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or anything else.

Who can see your data

  • Members of your team can see your team’s data, according to their role.
  • The PitDog administrator can see all data to run and support the service, for example to fix a broken race log.
  • Data is stored with Supabase (servers in the United States) and the app is hosted on GitHub Pages. They process it only to provide those services.

Deleting your data

Leave your team or ask your team owner to remove you. To delete your account and its data, email paul@neto.me; it will be removed within 30 days. Race logs you wrote stay with the team’s race history unless the team is deleted.
