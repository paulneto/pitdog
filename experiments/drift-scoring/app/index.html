DRIFTSCORE

Score your drifts with just your phone. No extra hardware needed.

How it works

  1. Mount your phone firmly in the car (dash or windscreen mount, any orientation). Not in your hand, not loose in a cupholder.
  2. Tap Start session and keep the car still for 3 seconds while it calibrates.
  3. Drive your runs. Include a few seconds of normal, straight driving between drifts so the app can re-align itself.
  4. Tap Stop to get your score: angle, speed, duration, smoothness and transitions.

Safety

No car handy?

Phone sensors aren't lab equipment. Scores are for fun and practice, not official judging.

Hold still…

Calibrating the gyro and finding which way is down. Keep the car stopped and the phone in its mount.

Every drift

Each drift is scored out of 100: angle 40 (full marks at 40°+ average), speed 25, duration 20 (full at 6 s+), smoothness 15, plus up to +10 for transitions (linking left↔right). The run score is the average of your best three.
