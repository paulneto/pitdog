# 24 Hours of Lemons: what PitDog uses
No full public rulebook was found. These come from [Lemons race pages](https://24hoursoflemons.com/race-2/?id=451). **Check your race page and the drivers' meeting.**

| Rule | In PitDog |
|---|---|
| Fuel only in the **hot pits or at track pumps**, never in the paddock during the race | pit-screen reminder |
| While fuelling: kill switch off, **nobody in the car**, drip pan, dedicated extinguisher person, full gear | pit-screen reminder |
| Hot pit only for fuelling, driver changes, cool-suit ice; no staging | info |
| Max stint / minimum stop | none official (stints of about 1 h recommended) → set your own max stint |
