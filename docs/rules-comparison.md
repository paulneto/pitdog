# Endurance series rules: comparison (as built into PitDog)
*Researched 27 Nov 2026 from each series' published rules. Event supplementals always override. **Check the supps before every event.***

| | Lucky Dog | ChampCar | WRL | AER | 24 Hours of Lemons | PCA Club Racing enduro |

## How PitDog uses this
Pick the **series per race** (New race → Series, or Plan → Race → Series rules). The preset sets the max stint, rest, minimum stop and whether it applies to fuel stops or all stops, the mandatory stop count or formula, the stop window, pits under FCY, fuel limit and race-clock style. You can still override any value for that race. The **at-a-glance** card shows on the new-race sheet and the Plan tab, with pit-wall reminders on the pit screen.

**Not covered:** "Enduro Elite": no series by that name was found. NASA WERC: the 2026 rules PDF wasn't accessible. SCCA enduros (5:00 fuel stops, no max stint per SCCA coverage). Can be added on request.
